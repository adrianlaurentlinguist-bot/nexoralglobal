How We Work

A managed process, not a single translator.

Every project moves through the same four stages, whether you're an LSP sending overflow work or a company localizing a product launch.

01

Scope & assign

We review the source content, confirm terminology and register, and assign the right linguists for the subject matter and language pair.

02

Translate or MTPE

Content is translated by hand or run through machine translation post-editing, depending on volume, timeline and use case.

03

Linguistic QA

A second linguist independently checks terminology, consistency, formatting and regional suitability before anything ships.

04

Deliver & debrief

Final files are delivered with a short QA summary, and terminology decisions are logged for consistency on future work.
